# 🎶 SDA Hymns – Advent Media Player

**Your digital companion for worship and praise.**  
SDA Hymns brings the *Seventh-day Adventist Church Hymnal* to your Android device, making it easy to access hymns in both English and Filipino (Philippine Edition). Designed for worship, devotion, and choir use, the app combines simplicity with powerful features.

---

## 📖 Features
- **Complete SDA Hymnal Collection**  
  Access hundreds of hymns with both English and Tagalog translations.
- **Dual Editions**  
  Includes *SDA Church Hymnal – Philippine Edition*, *Seventh-day Adventist Hymnal*, and *Sabbath Worship Responses*.
- **Search & Navigation**  
  Quickly find hymns by number or title with a built-in search bar.
- **Favorites**  
  Save your most-loved hymns for easy access.
- **Audio Accompaniment**  
  Play music for each hymn to support worship and choir practice.
- **Offline Access**  
  Hymns remain available even without internet connection.
- **Clean UI**  
  Minimalist design tailored for worship settings.

---

## 📲 Installation
1. Download the APK or install via Google Play (if available).  
2. Open the app and select your preferred hymnal edition.  
3. Start singing and worshiping with ease.
